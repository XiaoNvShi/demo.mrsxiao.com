FAQ

Use direct language and avoid unsupported technical claims.

How do I know which ARC hub fits my bike?

Check your frame and fork axle standard, brake mount, cassette/freehub type and spoke hole count. Use the Find Your Hub page if unsure.

What is the difference between HG, Micro Spline, XD and XDR?

They are cassette/freehub standards. The hub freehub body must match your cassette and drivetrain.

Can I convert my hub to another freehub body?

Some hub models can be converted with the correct freehub body and end caps. Confirm the exact model before ordering service parts.
