Questions

FAQ for ARC buyers.

How do I know whether I need HG, Micro Spline or XD?

Check your cassette and drivetrain system. HG is common for many Shimano/SRAM cassettes, Micro Spline is used for Shimano 12-speed MTB, and XD is used for SRAM XD cassettes.

Can a non-Boost hub be converted to Boost?

Some setups require dedicated hub shells while others may use adapters. ARC should confirm by model before buyers order conversion parts.

What should I check before ordering a hub?

Application, axle standard, brake system, freehub type, spoke hole count, flange requirement and rim drilling.

Do service parts fit all ARC hubs?

No. Freehub bodies, axles and end caps should be matched by model and production version.
