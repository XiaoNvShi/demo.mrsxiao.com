ARC C-end Site Planning Summary

Reference layout direction:
- Large creative landing hero, service/category cards, statistics, visual work grid, capability bars, testimonials and contact CTA.
- Rebuilt for ARC bicycle hub retail users in Europe and North America.

Pages generated:
1. index.html - homepage with hero, category cards, stats, featured hubs, real-scene sections, skills, testimonials and CTA.
2. shop-hubs.html - full hub catalogue with filter sidebar and retail product cards.
3. mtb-hubs.html - MTB hubs category page for Trail / Enduro / XC.
4. road-gravel-hubs.html - road and gravel category page.
5. service-parts.html - freehub bodies, axles, end caps, bearings and kits.
6. product-mt088.html - product detail template with gallery, options, specs and fitment CTA.
7. find-your-hub.html - fitment guide page.
8. technology.html - hub technology and standards page.
9. support.html - FAQ, warranty, shipping and returns.
10. about.html - brand story for ARC C-end site.
11. contact.html - support and dealer inquiry form.

Notes:
- No vector icon files or inline vector drawings are used.
- Real-scene JPG images are loaded from remote photo URLs; replace with ARC-owned images before launch.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS can be adapted to WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce or UEESHOP theme structure.
